This chapter follows the same recorded game from the save of 43:42 to the save of 45:20. The
author named its theme: **Batteries**. With the Auogs of chapter 16 the author built the battery
line north of the base, rebuilt it once after finding a better recipe, and brought the bus to it.
Times are game times. The numbers come from the game data, the recorder, the author's Factory
Planner draft 电池 (44:53), and the saves of 43:42 and 45:20 (`tools/inspect-save.ps1`, and
`tools/rate-probe` for one and three hours, with what-ifs); the author's fixes after the chapter
were measured on their saves of 45:25, 45:31, 45:40 and 45:52.

Conclusions:

- **The line makes 6 Batteries a minute, as many as its Chemical plant can.** The first came after
  45:10; at 45:20, 32 wait in the chest. Run on from the save it keeps 6 a minute for 40 minutes,
  then about 5.3: the last two Auog paddocks often wait for Water barrels. With five more Water
  barrels in each of them it keeps 6 for the whole hour. The author has since put 69 barrels in the
  loop, which is enough: on a belt the last paddock runs only from 50 barrels on (the author's
  count), and about 17 more are on the move. Then the Stone for the Moss farms held the line at
  about 5.6 a minute: one Mechanical inserter took it out of the Jaw crusher, 0.79 a second against
  0.86 needed. The author's final version (45:52) takes it out with two and moves it by a two-tile
  lane change onto the lane the Moss belt's end holds back: 6 Batteries a minute, measured.
- **The author's redesign: Ammonia into Cyanic acid.** The first draft left 180 Ammonia a minute
  without a user. A Quenching tower makes Cyanic acid from that Ammonia, Methane and Coal (the
  author's note at 44:07); now nothing is left over and a Battery takes 11.3 Urea instead of 21.3:
  3.43 paddocks instead of 6.46. The author built 4, with 16 Auogs.
- **The bus goes north** to the line: belts of Lead plates, Zinc plates, Coke and Antimony oxide,
  pipes of Gasoline, Molten glass and Steam. The line's Hydrogen, Coal gas and Tar go back into the
  bus through Check valves; the Carbon dioxide and Muddy sludge of its Melamine are voided on the
  spot.
- **What Batteries are for next**: one in each combinator (Cybersyn's requests), two in each
  Construction pynobot and roboport, three in every 12 Logistic science packs.
- **Left alone, the save runs down within the hour and does not recover**, and research stops with
  it. The cause is not the battery line but two sets of full chests. Six Ash chests at the Raw coal
  drills north of the base are full, so the Steam for the tar refinery fails, and with it Coke, Tar,
  Acetylene and the science packs; the author has since sent that Ash to the Ash separation. Then
  the 37 Kerogen chests at the Stone drills of chapter 16 are full too: after about 105 minutes the
  Stone bricks run out, and the pY1 packs, the Moss and the Batteries stop. The author has since put
  the Kerogen into two Deposits, full after about 3 hours 20 minutes.
- **The author's Underflow valves at the first coke columns (save of 45:31) stop those columns at
  once.** The Coal gas one waits for the bus pipe to fall under 80 %, while the bus's only other
  way out, the Overflow valve to the Syngas Gasifiers, waits for it to rise over 80 %: Tar falls
  from 36 to under 2 a second, Coke from 10 to under 2, Syngas to nothing, and the Batteries stop
  after 40 minutes. With that one valve a Pipe again everything runs, as the author has done by
  45:40; the Tar one stays.
- **A third timer: Creosote.** Its only user is Treated wood for the rail assembler. On the save of
  45:52 the Tin plates ran out after about 100 minutes, the rails slowed, the full Creosote pipe
  stood, and the Tar refining units stopped at about 140 minutes, Coke and the Batteries after them.
  With the Creosote drained (a stand-in for a Sinkhole) the three hours held. The Tin stopped because
  its bricks wait on the full left lane of the bus's brick belt (the author's finding); a lane
  balancer before they join is the author's fix.

## 1. What a Battery takes

3 Bolts, 3 Graphite, 3 Zinc plates and 30 Cyanic acid (data). The chain behind the two chemicals:

| 100 Cyanic acid + 50 Hydrogen | Quenching tower, 5 s | 100 Methane, 100 Ammonia, 20 Coal |
| 20 Melamine + 30 Carbon dioxide + 50 Muddy sludge | Fluidised bed reactor, 10 s | 200 Water, 20 Cyanic acid, 20 Ammonia |
| 30 Urea | Bioreactor, 8 s | 100 Liquid manure, 10 Zogna bacteria |

## 4. Six a minute, and the Water barrels

![The middle of the line at 45:52: at the top the Jaw crusher, the four Moss farms, the Washer, the Soil extractor and the greenhouse for Carbon dioxide; the belt below them brings Native flora and Moss; then the four Auog paddocks with four Auogs each; bottom left the Barreling Machine, from which the Water barrels run past the paddocks](../images/ch17-manure.jpg)

Run on from the save, the line made 0.100 Batteries a second for 40 minutes, then 0.090, 0.087,
0.080 and 0.090 in the last four 5-minute steps (`tools/rate-probe`, an hour). The Urea, Ammonia and
Cyanic acid in the pipes ran out, and the paddocks gave 0.334 Manure a second, not 0.38: they waited
for an ingredient 24 % of the time.

The ingredient is the Water barrel. The Barreling Machine fills barrels; a belt takes them past the
four paddocks, and the empty ones come back. At 45:20 the loop holds about 60 barrels: 20 in the
paddocks' input slots (10, 9, 1 and 0 from west to east), 20 empty on the belts, and up to 20 inside
the four crafts that are running. The first paddocks on the belt fill their slots first; the last
two wait. The Barreling Machine itself waited for Empty barrels 87 % of the hour.

**What-if** (`tools/rate-probe` with `add@x:y::water-barrel*5`, the player handing barrels in): five
Water barrels more in each of the last two paddocks, and the line made 0.100 a second in every step
of the hour, the paddocks working 82 %. Ten more in each did the same.

The author had reckoned 60 barrels, 4 paddocks × (5 in a running craft + 10 in the input), and
suspected the belts: a barrel on its way is in no paddock. **The author's test**, an endless-speed
loop (`tools/barrel-probe`, save of 45:31: every Empty barrel a paddock puts out becomes a Water
barrel at once and goes, as on the belt, to the first paddock from the Barreling Machine whose
input is not full; Native flora and Moss topped up, so that only the barrels count):

| Barrels in the loop | 40 | 45 | 49 | 50 | 60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paddocks 1–3 (working) | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| Paddock 4, the last on the belt | 0 % | 0 % | 0 % | 100 % | 100 % |

After the first ten minutes the fourth paddock never ran with 49 barrels or fewer. That is the
author's count: each of the first three keeps 15 (10 in the input, 5 in its craft) before a barrel
reaches the fourth, so the fourth runs from (5 + 10) × 3 + 5 = 50 barrels on. With 50, after the
battery line had stood for 20 minutes (the paddocks' Manure inserters stopped), all four started
again and none waited for barrels; the third stood "output full" for another 20 to 30 minutes
while its stored Manure was taken out. A first version of this test sent each barrel to the paddock
with the fewest and made even 40 barrels look enough; the author pointed out that a belt does not
share that way.

On the real belts the barrels on their way come on top: the loop of 60 kept 15 on the move on
average (14 on belts, the rest in inserters and outputs), so the inputs held 29 instead of 40.

**The author's fix** (45:20–45:25): more barrels in the loop, 69 in all. Measured on the save of
45:31 (`tools/barrel-probe`, an hour): with Moss and Native flora topped up, about 17 barrels were
on the move, the first three paddocks worked all the time and the fourth 94 to 98 %. After a
20-minute stop of the Manure inserters, while the paddocks stood full, 67 of the 69 barrels gathered
on the belts; in the ten minutes after it all four worked 91 to 94 %, then as before. As built, the
paddocks waited 19 to 23 % of the time, now for Moss, and Manure came to 0.36 a second: about 5.6
Batteries a minute.

**Moss: the Stone, not the farms.** Four Moss farms make up to 0.8 Moss a second and the draft
needs 0.69 (3.43 farms), so they have room. But the fourth of them, the last on their belt, lacked

The author suspected the Ammonia, which had no priority between the Melamine and the Quenching
tower; for a while an Overflow valve at (5.5, −396.5) gave it to the Melamine first (a Pipe again
by 45:40). But the Melamine was
never short (its reactor stood "output full" 90 % of the time or more in every hour measured, on the

**What-if** (save of 45:31, the Coal gas valve of section 8 a Pipe): that inserter an Inserter

**The cause, step by step** (from the saves and the timelines):

1. Six wooden chests on the Raw coal field north of the base, at (24, −87), (52, −70) and (54, −79),
   store the Ash of its Burner mining drills. All six are full (16,000 Ash); two of them filled

2. Behind the full chests, Burner mining drills stop with their Ash slot full (six at x 55 to 59
   alone). The belt at x 71.5 that they feed carried 1,437 Raw coal at 43:42 and 469 at 45:20.
3. That belt fuels the north row of the 24 Raw coal Boilers at (−42, 110): at 45:20, 8 of its 12
   have no fuel. Those Boilers' Steam goes past an Underflow valve at (−60.5, 110.5) into the long
   Steam pipe along the bus to the tar refinery at (−150, −100): three Tar processing units and three
   Destructive distillation columns. At 43:42 that pipe held Steam; at 45:20 it is empty, and the
   refinery works on the Steam stored near it.
4. The units' Tar refining bottoms take 100 Steam with 100 Tar, the columns' Coke recipe 100 Steam
   with 100 Pitch. When the stored Steam runs out (about 30 minutes), they slow; that Coke recipe
   and the Gasoline recipe from Anthracene oil (5 Coke besides) make most of
   the base's Coke, so Coke falls, then Acetylene, the lead drills and the science packs.

**Tests** (`tools/rate-probe` what-ifs, an hour each):

- The six Ash chests emptied every second (`empty@x:y`): the hour holds: Tar 34, Coke 9.4, packs
  as before (0.20 and 0.10), 89 MW at the end. The chests took 3.6 Ash a second.
- The author's test: an endless Steam source (`infinity-pipe`, 250 °C) behind an Underflow valve on
  the refinery's Steam pipe, so it only tops the pipe up: the hour holds the same way (Tar 34, Coke
  9.4), and the source gave 81 to 93 Steam a second.
- None of these changed the run-down: the Electrolyzers off, the battery line's Smelter off, its
  Wood column off, its Graphite furnace off, the new Overflow valves replaced by pipes, the Coke
  Splitter without priority, the Gasoline pump of the research Glassworks off, a Tailings pond on

- The save of 43:42, run for three hours, runs down too, later: Tar holds at 34 a second for two
  hours and twenty minutes, then falls (23 at 160 minutes, 11 at 180), and research stops at three
  hours.

**Fixes** (the author's choice):

- Empty the six chests by hand: that buys time, but they fill again.
- Give the field's Ash a lasting way out, about 3.6 a second: the Solid separators of the Ash
  separation, three of them at (−42, 97.5) beside those Boilers, waited for Ash 57 % of the hour.
- The author's idea, a Raw coal Steam block for the refinery: the test needed up to 93 Steam a
  second. A Boiler takes 3.7 MW and makes 7.5 Steam a second at 250 °C (data), so about 12 Boilers,
  14.5 Raw coal and 14.5 Ash a second; that block needs its Ash handled too.

**The author's fix** (45:20–45:25): the ten Ash chests on the field and their inserters taken up;
the field's Ash now goes on a new belt west along y −24 onto the bus's Ash belts at x −93, which run
to the Ash separation. Measured on the save of 45:25 (`tools/rate-probe`, three hours): the north

## 10. Key points

- Count what each product takes, then let the byproduct nobody uses become an input: the
  Quenching tower turns the spare Ammonia into Cyanic acid and halves the Auogs.
- A barrel loop on a belt serves its machines in order: the last of n runs only once the others
  each hold a full input and a running craft, (n − 1) × (input + craft) + one craft, and the
  barrels on the move come on top (the author's count: (5 + 10) × 3 + 5 = 50, plus about 17).
- One Mechanical inserter takes about 0.8 items a second out of a machine. Where a block needs more,
  that inserter is the limit, however many machines it feeds.
- A chest that stores a byproduct is a timer: the base runs until it is full. Six Ash chests at
  the Raw coal drills ended the Steam for the tar refinery, and with it Coke, Tar, Acetylene and
  research, within two hours and for good; 37 Kerogen chests at the Stone drills (every Stone mined
  brings a Kerogen) ended the Stone bricks, and with them the pY1 packs and the Moss.
- Two inputs on one belt that end in a lane filter: put each on its own lane. The author's lane
  change does it in two tiles with an underground entrance (three with plain belts).
- A byproduct with a single user ties the producer to that user: the tar refinery's Creosote went
  only into Treated wood for rails, so it ran only as fast as the rails were made.
- An Underflow valve feeding a pipe whose only other way out is an Overflow valve locks at 80 %:
  one passes only below, the other only above. Give a fluid priority with an Underflow valve only
  where something always draws the pipe down.
- To find the first cause, test what-ifs that each take one suspect away; when all fail alike, the
  cause is elsewhere. An endless source behind an Underflow valve tests a missing input and
  measures how much is missing.
